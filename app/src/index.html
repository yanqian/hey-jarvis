How sleep and wake work

When enabled, Hey Jarvis prevents automatic system sleep only while wake listening is genuinely active. The display may still turn off and the Mac may be locked. This can use more battery; explicit Sleep and closing a MacBook lid stop listening while asleep, then make one safe recovery attempt after wake. Shutdown still stops voice availability.

Local until you wake it. Wake phrase detection stays on this Mac. Conversation audio reaches OpenAI only after wake.
