## Observations

- Three wake-triggered Realtime sessions were recorded and all three contained
  `wake_confirmed`, exclusive wake-microphone close, browser microphone
  acquisition, transport/session creation, and final wake recovery.
- The first two sessions produced no audible response because near-end speech
  began about 250 ms before `host_connected` and the resulting incomplete turn
  closed through the semantic end tool. This is the separately scoped
  wake-to-ready first-utterance gap explicitly excluded from F073, not a
  wake-detector failure or built-in-speaker playback failure.
- The qualifying third session used `far_field` and output volume `0.3`.
  Its first audible response had `host_playback_started` followed by
  `host_playback_stopped` 5,557 ms later with no intervening
  `host_speech_started`, demonstrating continuous normal built-in-speaker
  playback without the former self-echo cancellation chain.
- Later in the same session, operator speech during the test produced
  `host_speech_started`, an active response completed as `cancelled` 37 ms
  later, and a subsequent response was created and completed. The operator
  confirmed that the final explicit ending utterance was intentional.
- The explicit ending invoked the semantic end tool, stopped browser media, and
  restored wake ownership with the wake microphone reopened 130 ms after the
  stop command.
- The user reported that the final run matched the expected interaction. The
  earlier apparent wake failures are accounted for by the distinct readiness
  gap above.
